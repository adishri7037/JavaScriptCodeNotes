6. Why is input validation important in console-based applications? Give examples of validation rules.
7. What is modular programming? Why should a large program be divided into focused functions?

9. What edge cases should be tested in a Student Record Manager? Explain at least five.
10. Explain the design of the Student Record Manager. Identify the functions responsible for validation, searching, calculations and reporting.
